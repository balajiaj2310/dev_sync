Google OAuth Setup Required

Google Sign-In is equipped with real server verification at /api/v1/auth/google. To activate real Google OAuth in your local environment:

  1. Create an OAuth 2.0 Client ID in the Google Cloud Console.
  2. Add http://localhost:8000 to Authorized JavaScript origins.
  3. Set your Client ID in your terminal or .env file:
export GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID="YOUR_CLIENT_ID.apps.googleusercontent.com"

Then restart server.py. The frontend will immediately enable real Google Identity Services authentication without mock or fake success.
