Privacy Policy

Notice — Draft Policy for Review: This document is a draft privacy policy prepared for testing and preview evaluation of the DevSync platform. It outlines the actual data practices currently supported by this application and does not make claims regarding third-party certifications or unintegrated systems.

1. Information We Collect

DevSync collects information you directly provide when registering an account, configuring your profile, or contributing to repositories:

  • Account Registration: Your full name, email address used for OTP verification, username, and password (securely hashed using Django’s cryptographic password hasher).
  • Developer Profile: Bio, skills, website URL, location, and social links that you choose to provide in profile editing.
  • Repository & Collaboration Data: Repository names, commits, pull requests, issues, comments, and team membership assignments created within the application.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is strictly used to deliver and improve platform functionality:

  • Verifying ownership of your email address via secure one-time verification codes (OTP).
  • Authenticating your session and safeguarding your account against unauthorized access.
  • Rendering your public developer profile and displaying repository contributions to your collaborators.
  • Enabling team owners to allocate roles and coordinate projects within DevSync squads.

3. Local Storage & Cookies

DevSync uses client-side Web Storage (localStorage) to maintain your active authentication session token and application UI preferences (such as dark/light theme state and collapsed navigation bars). We do not use third-party advertising tracking cookies.

4. Data Sharing & Visibility

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. Public repositories and public profile information are viewable by members of the DevSync developer community. Private repositories and team-restricted workspaces are visible only to authorized team members and owners designated for that project.

5. Data Security

Passwords are never stored in plaintext and are hashed using industry-standard PBKDF2 cryptographic algorithms. Verification OTP codes are time-limited, rate-limited, and validated exclusively on the backend server.
