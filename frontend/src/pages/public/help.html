How can we help you build?

Explore guides for account setup, email OTP verification, repository collaboration, and squad management.

Email OTP Verification

DevSync uses secure email one-time passwords (OTP) to verify ownership of your email address during registration before setting up your password. OTP codes expire in 10 minutes.

Repositories & Branches

Create public or private repositories, review pull request diffs, track commit history, and configure branch protection rules for your engineering squad.

Team Management & Roles

Project owners possess exclusive authority to assign member roles (Owner, Maintainer, Contributor), allocate squad projects, and manage team membership.
